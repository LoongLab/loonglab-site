What we do

We build products, not demos. Each one is designed to be used every day, to respect your machine, and to explain what it is doing. We ship when a product is ready rather than announcing a roadmap.

LoongCode

LoongCode is our first product: a terminal coding agent that works directly in your repository. It reads the code, makes changes, runs your commands, and verifies its own work.

Its central advantage is cutting token consumption. Long tasks are the hard case for a coding agent: the context window fills, the agent loses the thread, and every request costs more. LoongCode manages context at every boundary so long sessions cost less and stay coherent.
